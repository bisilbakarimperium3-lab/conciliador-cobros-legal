Privacy Policy / Política de privacidad — Conciliador cobros

Conciliador cobros is an internal tool of the business activity of María Estefani Mas Segura (self-employed, online sale of collectibles). With her express authorisation, it reads, in read-only mode (no payments are initiated), the incoming payments of her own bank accounts (Revolut, PayPal and, where applicable, BBVA) through the Enable Banking service, in order to match them with orders awaiting payment. Data processed: date, amount, transaction identifier, the payer's name (personal data of a third party, normally the buyer, used only to identify their order) and the payment reference. The matching may be assisted by artificial intelligence tools. The data are not sold, published or used for advertising, and are not shared except with the banking service provider (Enable Banking) and the technical providers strictly necessary for the processing. They are kept as long as needed for the accounting management of the orders. The person who maintains the system does so on her behalf and under her instructions. Access is granted for a maximum of 180 days and can be revoked at any time from the bank or from the Enable Banking control panel. Contact and exercise of rights (access, rectification, erasure): ems.estefania.ems@hotmail.com.
